How Provably Fair works

Verify on bc.game

Every BC.GAME Originals result can be recomputed by anyone. Here’s the idea in three steps.

1Before you betWe commit to a server seed and show you its hash.
→
2You add your seedYour client seed (and a counter) go into every result.
→
3After the roundWe reveal the server seed. The hash must match — and the result must recompute.
